© 2026 Tiki Taka Hostel & Pub Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Tiki Taka Hostel & Pub (A. Goštauto g. 8, Naujamiestis, Vilnius) is responsible for the data you send us through this website.

What we collect

Only what you type in the contact form: your name, your email or phone, the topic and your message. If you write to us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy terms also apply.

Why

To answer your enquiry (a booking, a table, a group or event). We do not use it for anything else and we do not sell it.

Who sees it

Our team, and the service that delivers the form messages to us (for example an email or form provider), which processes it only for that purpose.

How long

Only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting booking, and as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, at any time. Write to us at our email. You can also complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).
